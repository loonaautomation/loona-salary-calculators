Saudi End of Service Calculator

Calculate your end of service benefits (EOSB) under Articles 84 and 85 of the Saudi Labour Law — including the resignation scale that many calculators get wrong. Saudi Arabia has no income tax; this award is your end-of-service entitlement.

In short: Saudi end of service benefits pay half a month’s last wage (basic salary plus fixed allowances) per year for the first five years and a full month per year after that, with no cap. If you resign, Article 85 pays one third after 2–5 years, two thirds after 5–10 years, the full award after 10 years, and nothing under 2 years.

How it is calculated

Articles 84–85 of the Saudi Labour Law (Royal Decree M/51):

  • The base is your last wage: basic salary plus fixed allowances (housing, transport) as defined in your contract.
  • Article 84 (employer ends the contract, contract expiry, retirement): half a month's wage per year for the first 5 years, one full month's wage per year after the fifth. Part-years are pro-rated. There is no cap.
  • Article 85 (you resign): under 2 years = 0; 2 to under 5 years = ⅓ of the award; 5 to under 10 years = ⅔; 10+ years = full award.
  • Article 87 cases (force majeure; a female worker resigning within 6 months of marriage or 3 months of childbirth) pay the full award despite resignation. Termination for cause under Article 80 pays nothing.

Worked example: last wage SAR 10,000, 7 years, employer-ended → (10,000 × 0.5 × 5) + (10,000 × 1 × 2) = SAR 25,000 + SAR 20,000 = SAR 45,000. If the same employee resigned, Article 85 pays ⅔ of that = SAR 30,000.

Frequently asked questions

Which wage is used — basic only or basic plus allowances?

Basic plus fixed allowances (housing, transport) as written in your contract. This is a key difference from the UAE, where only basic salary counts.

I resign after 3 years — how much do I get?

One third of the full Article 84 award (Article 85). Before 2 years it is zero; from 5 years it is two thirds; from 10 years, the full award.

Is there a maximum award?

No statutory cap exists under Articles 84–85.

When is the full award paid despite resignation?

In Article 87 cases (force majeure; marriage/childbirth windows for female workers) and after 10+ years of service.

Did the 2025 amendments change the formula?

No — the February 2025 amendments changed procedures (probation, notice, Qiwa resignation), not the Articles 84–85 amounts.

How it works — the formula

First the calculator builds the full Article 84 award on your last wage (basic plus fixed allowances): half a month's wage for each of the first 5 years plus one full month's wage for each year after the fifth, with part-years pro-rated (service is measured in days ÷ 365.25) and no maximum. If the employer ended the contract, it expired, you retired, or an Article 87 case applies, that full award is paid. If you resigned, Article 85 pays a share of it: nothing under 2 years, one third from 2 to under 5 years, two thirds from 5 to under 10 years, and the full award from 10 years.

Worked example

Last wage SAR 12,000, 8 years of service. Full award = (12,000 × 0.5 × 5) + (12,000 × 1 × 3) = SAR 30,000 + SAR 36,000 = SAR 66,000 if the employer ends the contract. If the same employee resigns, 8 years falls in the 5-to-under-10 band, so Article 85 pays two thirds: SAR 44,000.

Frequently asked questions

How are part-years counted?

Pro-rata. Service is measured in days ÷ 365.25, so a half year beyond the fifth year adds half a month's wage (the after-five rate is one month per year), and a half year inside the first five adds a quarter month's wage.

I resign after 4 years — how much do I get?

One third of the full award. On a last wage of SAR 12,000 the full award after 4 years is SAR 24,000 (4 × half a month), so resignation pays SAR 8,000.

Is the award based on my last wage or an average?

Your last wage — basic plus fixed allowances as written in your contract at the end of service. Earlier, lower salaries do not dilute it, which is why a late-career raise lifts the award for all your years.

What if I am dismissed for cause under Article 80?

Nothing is payable, which is why the reasons list in this calculator has no Article 80 option: the award is zero by law in that case.

Is the end-of-service award the same as a pension?

No. This is the one-off lump sum under Articles 84–85, paid when employment ends. GOSI social insurance is a separate system — see the Saudi Salary Calculator for the monthly GOSI contributions.
