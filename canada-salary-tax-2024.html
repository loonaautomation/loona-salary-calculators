How it works — the formula

Income tax is computed progressively: 15% on income from 0 to 55,867; 20.5% on income from 55,867 to 111,733; 26% on income from 111,733 to 173,205; 29% on income from 173,205 to 246,752; 33% on income from 246,752 and above. A credit of C$2,356 is deducted from the tax. No social-security deduction is modelled (see the notes for what is excluded). Net pay is gross minus income tax and the social contributions modelled. This is an estimate for a single earner with no other income and no dependents — your actual pay may differ.
