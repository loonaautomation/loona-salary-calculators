UAE Final Settlement Calculator

Your complete exit package in one calculation: Article 51 gratuity, pay for unused annual leave, and notice-period compensation — itemised, so you can check every line against your HR statement.

In short: a UAE final settlement is your gratuity (21 days of basic wage per year for the first five years, 30 days after) plus unused-leave pay on the basic wage, plus notice-period pay on the full wage if notice was not served. Everything is due within 14 days of your contract ending.

How it is calculated

  • Gratuity (Article 51): daily basic wage = monthly basic ÷ 30; 21 days per year for the first 5 years, 30 days per year after; at least 1 year of service required; capped at 2 years' wage. Resignation does not reduce it.
  • Unused-leave pay: annual leave is 30 calendar days per year (Article 29). Standard practice pays untaken leave on the basic wage: (basic ÷ 30) × unused days. This component is contract-dependent — the basis used here is the common practitioner method, so confirm it against your contract.
  • Notice-period pay: contracts carry a 30–90 day notice period (Article 43). If your employer ends the contract without the notice being served, compensation equals the wage for that period, calculated here on the full wage: (total wage ÷ 30) × notice days owed. If you are the one who did not serve notice, the same amount may be deducted instead — enter 0 in that case.

Worked example: basic AED 10,000, total wage AED 15,000, 7 years of service, 10 unused leave days, 30 notice days owed → gratuity AED 55,000 + leave pay AED 3,333.33 + notice pay AED 15,000 = AED 73,333.33.

Your final salary up to the last working day, and any agreed extras (repatriation ticket, end-of-service savings scheme payouts), come on top of the three lines above. DIFC and ADGM have their own schemes.

Frequently asked questions

What is included in a UAE final settlement?

Typically gratuity under Article 51, pay for earned but untaken annual leave, and notice-period compensation if notice was not served — plus your salary up to the last working day.

How is unused-leave pay calculated?

Standard practice pays it on the basic wage: (monthly basic ÷ 30) × unused days. The basis is contract-dependent, so check your contract or HR statement.

How does notice-period pay work?

Notice is 30–90 days under Article 43. If your employer ends the contract without notice being served, you are owed the wage for that period, normally on the full wage.

When must my employer pay?

Within 14 days of the contract ending (Article 53).

Does resigning reduce any part of it?

No — since February 2022 resignation does not reduce gratuity, and leave already earned is still payable.

How it works — the formula

The total is three separate components added together, each on its own wage basis. Gratuity (Article 51) uses the basic wage: (basic ÷ 30) × 21 days × the first 5 years + (basic ÷ 30) × 30 days × later years, capped at 24 × monthly basic, and zero if service is under one year. Unused-leave pay uses the basic wage too: (basic ÷ 30) × unused days — the standard-practice basis, which your contract can vary. Notice pay uses the full wage: (total wage ÷ 30) × notice days owed to you. Service years are measured from your dates in days ÷ 365.25, so part-years count pro-rata in the gratuity.

Worked example

Basic salary AED 10,000, total wage AED 15,000, 4 years of service, 12 unused leave days and 30 notice days owed. Gratuity = (10,000 ÷ 30) × 21 × 4 = AED 28,000. Leave pay = (10,000 ÷ 30) × 12 = AED 4,000. Notice pay = (15,000 ÷ 30) × 30 = AED 15,000. Final settlement total = AED 47,000.

Frequently asked questions

Why is leave pay on basic salary but notice pay on the full wage?

That is how this calculator implements the two different rules: untaken leave is settled at the basic-wage daily rate (standard practice, and contract-dependent), while compensation for an unserved notice period is the wage for that period, calculated on your full wage.

I served my notice period — what do I enter?

Zero notice days. Notice pay only arises when your employer ends the contract without the notice being served. If you are the one who did not serve notice, the same amount may be deducted from you instead — that case is not modelled here, so enter 0.

Is my final month's salary inside this total?

No. The total covers gratuity, unused leave and notice pay only. Your salary up to the last working day — and any agreed extras such as a repatriation ticket — are paid on top of it.

What shows if I worked less than one year?

The gratuity line is zero, because Article 51 needs a full year of service. Leave pay and notice pay are still calculated from whatever days you enter for them.

How does the two-year cap appear in this total?

It limits only the gratuity component, to 24 × your monthly basic. On a basic of AED 10,000, the gratuity inside the settlement can never exceed AED 240,000 however long the service; leave and notice pay are added on top of the capped figure.
