How it works — the formula

Income tax is computed progressively: 0% on income from 0 to 40,000; 10% on income from 40,000 to 55,000; 15% on income from 55,000 to 70,000; 20% on income from 70,000 to 200,000; 22.5% on income from 200,000 to 400,000; 25% on income from 400,000 to 1,200,000; 27.5% on income from 1,200,000 and above. Social contributions: 11.00% of gross pay. Net pay is gross minus income tax and the social contributions modelled. This is an estimate for a single earner with no other income and no dependents — your actual pay may differ.
