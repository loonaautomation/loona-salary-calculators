Netherlands Salary Calculator — Net Salary 2026

Your Dutch gross-to-net for tax year 2026: Box 1 brackets, the general and labour tax credits, the 8% holiday allowance, and the 30% ruling (with the 2027 change to 27% built in as an option).

In short: Dutch employees pay Box 1 tax of 35.75% up to €38,883, 37.56% up to €78,426 and 49.5% above that (2026), reduced by the general tax credit (up to €3,115) and the labour tax credit (up to €5,685). Salaries usually include 8% holiday allowance, and expats with the 30% ruling can receive up to 30% of salary tax-free.

How it is calculated

  • Box 1 brackets 2026: 35.75% up to €38,883 · 37.56% from €38,883 to €78,426 · 49.50% above €78,426 (rates include national-insurance premiums).
  • Holiday allowance: at least 8% of gross. If your quoted salary includes it, the calculator shows it separately; if not, 8% is added on top.
  • 30% ruling: the chosen percentage of salary is paid tax-free. It applies only to salary up to €262,000 (maximum allowance €78,600 in 2026) and your taxable salary after the allowance must stay above the salary norm — €48,013 in 2026, or €36,497 if under 30 with a qualifying master's. If the full percentage would breach the norm, this calculator reduces the allowance to the largest amount the norm allows and tells you. From 1 January 2027 the percentage drops to 27%, except for rulings applied before 1 January 2024, which keep 30%.
  • Tax credits (subtracted from the tax, never below zero): general tax credit max €3,115, reduced by 6.398% of income above €29,736; labour tax credit max €5,685 at €45,592 of income, reduced by 6.51% above that (gone at €132,920).
  • Pension: employee pension contributions are scheme-specific, not statutory. If you enter a percentage, it is deducted from taxable salary.

Worked example: €60,000 gross, no ruling, no pension → tax €15,906.47 → net €44,093.53 per year (€3,674.46 per month). The same salary with the 30% ruling: the norm limits the tax-free allowance to €11,987 (so that taxable salary stays at €48,013), tax falls to €9,856.87 and net rises to €50,143.13 per year.

Frequently asked questions

What is the 30% ruling?

An expat scheme: up to 30% of salary paid tax-free for up to 5 years, if you were recruited from abroad, lived 150+ km from the Dutch border, and your post-allowance taxable salary exceeds the norm (€48,013 in 2026; €36,497 under-30 with a master's).

What changes in 2027?

The maximum drops to 27% for rulings first applied from 2024; pre-2024 rulings keep 30%. Use the selector above to preview either case.

Is holiday allowance taxed?

Yes — it is part of your taxable salary, usually withheld at a special rate when paid in May. This calculator includes it in the annual figures either way.

Why does my real payslip differ?

Pension contributions through your employer's scheme (enter your % above), a company car, and other benefits change a real payslip. This tool is an estimate on salary alone.

What are the 2026 brackets?

35.75% to €38,883; 37.56% to €78,426; 49.50% above — then both tax credits reduce the bill.

How it works — the formula

Start with the full gross: if the quoted salary excludes the 8% holiday allowance, the total is salary × 1.08; if it already includes the allowance, the allowance inside it is total − total ÷ 1.08. From the total, subtract any employee pension contribution and — if you have the 30% ruling — the tax-free allowance. The remainder is taxed under the 2026 Box 1 brackets: 35.75% up to €38,883, 37.56% up to €78,426 and 49.50% above that. The general tax credit (maximum €3,115, phased out at 6.398% of income above €29,736) and the labour tax credit (maximum €5,685 at €45,592 of income, phased out at 6.51% above that) are then subtracted from the tax, never below zero. Net = total gross − pension − tax.

Worked example

Gross €85,000 including holiday allowance (so €6,296.30 of it is the allowance), pension contribution 5%, 30% ruling for 2026. Pension: €4,250. Tax-free allowance: the full 30% = €25,500 — the salary norm does not limit it here, because the taxable salary stays above €48,013. Taxable salary: 85,000 − 4,250 − 25,500 = €55,250. Bracket tax €20,048.12, minus general credit €1,482.61 and labour credit €5,056.26, gives tax of €13,509.24. Net: €67,240.76 a year, €5,603.40 a month. Without the ruling, the same salary nets €54,242.81 — so the ruling is worth €12,997.95 a year in this case.

Frequently asked questions

Why is the holiday allowance inside an €85,000 salary only €6,296, not €6,800?

Because the 8% is calculated on the salary excluding the allowance. If €85,000 already includes it, the allowance is 85,000 − 85,000 ÷ 1.08 = €6,296.30. A straight 8% of €85,000 (€6,800) would double-count: it treats the allowance itself as salary that also earns allowance.

My employer quotes a monthly salary — how do I use this calculator?

Multiply the monthly figure by 12 first, then check whether it includes the 8% holiday allowance — most Dutch quotes do; if yours does not, leave the "includes holiday allowance" box unticked and the calculator adds the 8% on top. Enter any pension percentage your scheme deducts from your pay.

Are Dutch social-security premiums deducted on top of the income tax?

There is no separate line: below AOW pension age, the Box 1 bracket rates already include the national-insurance premiums — 27.65 points of the 35.75% first-bracket rate are premiums. Dutch payroll withholds tax and premiums as one combined amount, and so does this calculator.

I am over the AOW pension age — are these figures right for me?

No. The brackets and credits used here are the below-AOW versions for 2026. Once you pass AOW age you stop paying the AOW premium, the first-bracket rate drops and the credits change, so your real net would be higher than the figure shown.

Does this calculator work for a part-time salary?

Yes — enter your actual part-time annual gross. The brackets and both tax credits apply to your real income, so a part-timer keeps more of the credits and a lower average rate than the same hourly wage worked full-time. Dutch payroll tables contain no full-time-equivalent conversion.
