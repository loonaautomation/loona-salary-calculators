Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use Loona Salary & Tax Calculators.

Your calculations stay on your device

Every calculator on this site runs entirely in your web browser. The salary figures, dates and other details you type in are never sent to us, never stored on a server, and never shared. We could not look at your calculation even if we wanted to — there is no database and no account system. This site has no sign-up, no login and no forms that collect personal information.

Analytics: Google Analytics 4 and Microsoft Clarity

Like most websites, we use analytics tools to understand which pages are useful and where visitors get stuck, so we can improve the calculators:

  • Google Analytics 4 (Google LLC) collects aggregated usage statistics — for example which calculators are visited, approximate region, device type and how long pages are used. It uses cookies to distinguish visits.
  • Microsoft Clarity (Microsoft Corporation) provides behavioural analytics such as heatmaps and interaction summaries that show us how pages are used, so confusing layouts can be fixed.

These services process usage data under their own privacy policies. They do not receive the numbers you enter into the calculators, which never leave your device.

Advertising: Google AdSense

This site is free and is supported by advertising served by Google AdSense. Google and its advertising partners use cookies to serve ads, including ads personalised to your interests based on your visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables Google and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

  • You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings.
  • You can also opt out of many third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info.
  • Read how Google uses data from sites that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Opting out does not remove ads; it makes them non-personalised.

Cookies and your browser controls

The analytics and advertising services above may set cookies in your browser. You can block or delete cookies at any time in your browser settings; the calculators will still work, because they need no cookies at all. If you email us, we receive your email address and the content of your message, and we use them only to reply.

Children, changes and contact

This site is a general-audience financial tool and is not directed at children. We may update this policy when the services we use change; the current version will always be on this page with its date at the top. Questions about privacy can be sent to loonaautomation@gmail.com.
