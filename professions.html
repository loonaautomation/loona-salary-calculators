How it is calculated

Half of the workers in each occupation earn more than the median, and half earn less. The median is the middle of the pay range — not the average, which a few very high earners pull up.

Each occupation page shows the median, the bottom 10% and the top 10% pay, and what each is worth after federal income tax, Social Security and Medicare. The ten most-searched occupations also have a page for each of the ten most populous states.

Source: U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, Occupational Employment and Wage Statistics, May 2025 estimates, national, all industries.

Frequently asked questions

What is the median salary in the US?

It depends on the occupation — the pages below give the median for 60 occupations, from $31,200 to $244,180 a year.

Where do these figures come from?

The U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, Occupational Employment and Wage Statistics (OEWS), May 2025 estimates — national, all industries. Each occupation page cites its SOC code and source page.

Why are some occupations missing?

This hub covers 60 of the 82 occupations in the BLS set we verified — the most-searched and most-common ones. The 22 not covered are the lowest-profile occupations in the set; nothing was invented for them.
