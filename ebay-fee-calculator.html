How it is calculated

eBay's final value fee has two parts: a percentage of the total amount of the sale (item price + shipping you charge the buyer) plus a flat per-order fee. For most categories the percentage is 13.6%, and the per-order fee is $0.30 when the order total is $10 or less and $0.40 when it is over $10. Sellers rated Below Standard pay an extra 5% on the percentage part.

Frequently asked questions

What percentage does eBay take from a sale?

For most categories eBay charges a final value fee of 13.6% of the total amount of the sale — item price plus shipping charged — plus a per-order fee of $0.30 on orders of $10 or less and $0.40 on orders over $10. Some categories (for example certain electronics, media and clothing price bands) use different percentages, which is why the rate in this calculator is editable.

Why are there two different per-order fees, $0.30 and $0.40?

eBay charges $0.30 per order when the total amount of the sale is $10 or less, and $0.40 per order when it is over $10. On very cheap items that difference is a big share of the fee, so this calculator picks the right one automatically from your order total — and both amounts are editable if eBay changes them.

What is the below-standard seller fee?

If your seller performance falls to Below Standard, eBay adds 5 percentage points to the final value fee percentage (so 13.6% becomes 18.6%) until your performance recovers. Turn it on with the seller-performance selector above to see what it costs you on each sale.

Does eBay charge its fee on shipping as well?

Yes. The final value fee percentage applies to the total amount of the sale, which includes the shipping you charge the buyer — so free-shipping listings do not avoid the fee, the percentage simply applies to the item price instead.

Do I also pay listing (insertion) fees?

Most casual sellers get a monthly allowance of free listings (250 zero-insertion-fee listings a month for most US sellers without a Store subscription), after which insertion fees of around $0.35 per listing apply. This calculator covers the final value fee, which is by far the largest cost on a completed sale; promoted-listing advertising fees are separate and depend on the ad rate you choose.

How it works — the formula

The calculator first adds up the total amount of the sale: total = item price + shipping charged. The percentage fee = total × (final value fee % + below-standard extra %, when switched on). The per-order fee is the “up to $10” amount when total ≤ $10 and the “over $10” amount otherwise. Total fees = percentage fee + per-order fee, and profit = total − total fees − your item cost − your shipping cost. Results are rounded to two decimals for display.

Frequently asked questions

Is the eBay fee charged on sales tax too?

Where eBay collects sales tax from the buyer, the final value fee is calculated on the total including that tax in many US states, because the fee is based on the total amount of the sale. The exact treatment varies; if it matters to your margins, add the tax-inclusive total as the item amount and compare.

What if my category has a different rate?

Select “Custom rate” and type your category's percentage into the rate field — the rest of the maths (per-order fee, below-standard extra, profit) works exactly the same.
