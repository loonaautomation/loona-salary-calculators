Oman Net Salary Calculator

Oman charges no personal income tax in 2026. Omani citizens contribute 7.5% to the Social Protection Fund; expatriate employees currently have no deduction at all.

In short: Oman has no personal income tax today — on a OMR 18,000.00 gross salary an expatriate nets the full OMR 18,000.00. Omani citizens pay 7.5% into the Social Protection Fund. (Oman has legislated a 5% income tax on earnings above OMR 42,000, but it starts on 1 January 2028 — it does not apply yet.)

How it is calculated

  • Income tax: Oman levies no personal income tax in 2026. A new law introduces a 5% tax on annual income above OMR 42,000 from 1 January 2028 — the first personal income tax in the Gulf — but nothing is deducted before then.
  • Social Protection Fund (citizens): Omani employees contribute 7.5% of salary to the Social Protection Fund (the employer adds 11%) for old-age, disability and survivors' cover. The reformed law extends some branches to expatriate employees in phases, but the expatriate old-age contribution was not yet in force as of November 2026, so expatriates currently pay nothing.
  • End-of-service gratuity: expatriates accrue a gratuity under the Oman Labour Law — broadly one month of basic salary per year of service in the standard cases — paid when the job ends, not deducted monthly. Use the Oman Gratuity Calculator linked below.

Worked example: an Omani citizen on OMR 18,000.00 gross pays 7.5% = OMR 1,350 to the Social Protection Fund and nets OMR 16,650 a year. An expatriate on the same salary nets the full OMR 18,000.00.

Frequently asked questions

Is there income tax in Oman?

Not yet. Oman has no personal income tax in 2026. A 5% income tax on earnings above OMR 42,000 a year has been legislated and takes effect on 1 January 2028, making Oman the first Gulf state with a personal income tax — but no salary is taxed before that date.

Do expatriates pay social insurance in Oman?

Currently no employee contribution is deducted from expatriate salaries. Oman's Social Protection Law extends parts of the scheme to expatriates in phases, and the expatriate old-age branch has been legislated but was not yet in force as of November 2026. Check again before relying on this for a 2027+ contract.

What does the Omani citizen 7.5% contribution cover?

It is the employee share paid into the Social Protection Fund for the old-age, disability and death branch; the employer adds 11%. Other branches (such as work injury) are employer-funded.

What end-of-service benefit do expatriates get in Oman?

A gratuity under the Labour Law (Royal Decree 53/2023), based on basic salary and years of service, paid as a lump sum when employment ends. It is not deducted from monthly pay — estimate it with our Oman Gratuity Calculator.

Will the 2028 income tax change my net salary a lot?

Only higher earners are affected: the 5% rate applies only to the slice of annual income above OMR 42,000, so even on OMR 60,000 the tax would be 5% of OMR 18,000 = OMR 900 a year. This calculator uses the 2026 rules, under which the tax is zero.

How it works — the formula

Net = gross − income tax − social contributions. In Oman in 2026 the income tax term is zero for everyone, and the contribution term is zero for expatriates, so net = gross for expatriate employees. For an Omani citizen, net = gross × (1 − 0.075) = gross × 0.925 after the 7.5% Social Protection Fund employee contribution. From 2028 a further term appears for high earners: 5% of the income above OMR 42,000.

Worked example

Expatriate, gross OMR 18,000.00 a year. Income tax: OMR 0. Social Protection Fund: OMR 0 (no expatriate employee contribution in force). Net = OMR 18,000.00 a year — OMR 1,500.00 a month. An Omani citizen on the same salary pays 7.5% × OMR 18,000.00 = OMR 1,350 and nets OMR 16,650 a year (OMR 1,387.50 a month).

Frequently asked questions

Why is Oman introducing an income tax in 2028?

To diversify state revenue away from oil and gas. The design is deliberately gentle: a high OMR 42,000 threshold (roughly the top few percent of earners) and a single 5% rate above it.

Are employer contributions deducted from my salary?

No. The employer's 11% Social Protection Fund contribution for Omani staff is paid on top of your salary and never reduces your net pay.

Does the gratuity depend on my basic salary?

Yes — like most Gulf gratuities it is built on basic salary and completed years of service, so two packages with the same total but different basic/allowance splits produce different gratuities. Monthly net pay, however, is the full package either way.

I am signing a multi-year contract — should I model the 2028 tax?

If you earn above OMR 42,000 and your contract runs past 1 January 2028, yes: budget for 5% of the excess over OMR 42,000 from 2028. Below that threshold nothing changes.
