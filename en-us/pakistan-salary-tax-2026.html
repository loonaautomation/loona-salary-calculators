How it works — the formula

Income tax is computed progressively: 0% on income from 0 to 600,000; 1% on income from 600,000 to 1,200,000; 11% on income from 1,200,000 to 2,200,000; 23% on income from 2,200,000 to 3,200,000; 30% on income from 3,200,000 to 4,100,000; 35% on income from 4,100,000 and above. No social-security deduction is modeled (see the notes for what is excluded). Net pay is gross minus income tax and the social contributions modeled. This is an estimate for a single earner with no other income and no dependents — your actual pay may differ.
