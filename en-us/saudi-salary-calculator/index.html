Saudi Salary Calculator — GOSI Net Pay

Saudi Arabia has no income tax, so your net pay comes down to one deduction: GOSI social insurance. It applies to Saudi nationals only — expatriate employees pay 0%.

In short: expats keep 100% of their Saudi salary. Saudi employees pay GOSI on basic salary plus housing allowance (floored at SAR 1,500, capped at SAR 45,000): 9.75% if first registered before July 3, 2024, or the new-system rate — 10.75% from July 1, 2026 — if registered later.

How it is calculated

  • No income tax is charged on employment income in Saudi Arabia, for any nationality.
  • GOSI employee deduction (Saudis only) = contributory wage × employee rate. The contributory wage is basic salary + housing allowance, minimum SAR 1,500 and maximum SAR 45,000 per month.
  • Old system (first registered before July 3, 2024): 9% pension + 0.75% SANED unemployment = 9.75%.
  • New system (registered on or after July 3, 2024, Social Insurance Law RD M/273): the pension share rises 0.5 points every July 1 — 9.5% in 2025-26, 10% from July 2026, 10.5% in 2027, 11% from 2028 — plus 0.75% SANED, giving totals of 10.25%, 10.75%, 11.25% and 11.75%.
  • Expats: employee deduction is 0%; the employer pays 2% occupational hazards on the same contributory wage.

Worked example: Saudi employee on the new system (2026-27), basic SAR 10,000 + housing SAR 5,000 → contributory wage SAR 15,000 × 10.75% = SAR 1,612.50 deducted; net pay = SAR 13,387.50. An expat on the same package nets the full SAR 15,000.

Frequently asked questions

Is there income tax on salaries in Saudi Arabia?

No — there is no personal income tax on employment income for anyone. GOSI is the only employee-side deduction, and it applies to Saudi nationals only.

How much GOSI do expats pay?

Zero. The employer alone pays a 2% occupational-hazards contribution for non-Saudi staff.

What salary is GOSI calculated on?

Basic salary plus housing allowance only, floored at SAR 1,500 and capped at SAR 45,000 a month. Transport and other allowances are outside the base.

Why are there two Saudi GOSI rates?

The new Social Insurance Law (for registrations from July 3, 2024) raises the pension share gradually: employee totals run 10.25% (2025-26), 10.75% (from July 1, 2026), 11.25% (2027-28) and 11.75% (from July 2028). Earlier registrations stay at 9.75%.

What does my employer pay on top?

For Saudis, the pension share again, plus 0.75% SANED and 2% hazards — 11.75% total on the old system, 12.75% on the new system from July 2026. For expats, just the 2% hazards branch.

How it works — the formula

There is no income tax, so for Saudi employees the only deduction is GOSI: deduction = contributory wage × employee rate. The contributory wage is basic salary plus housing allowance only, floored at SAR 1,500 and capped at SAR 45,000 a month. The employee rate is a pension share plus 0.75% SANED unemployment insurance: 9.75% in total on the old system (registered before July 3, 2024), and on the new system 10.25% for Jul 2025 – Jun 2026, 10.75% for Jul 2026 – Jun 2027 (the option selected by default), 11.25% for Jul 2027 – Jun 2028 and 11.75% from Jul 2028. Net pay = gross − your deduction. The employer's separate cost (the pension share again + 0.75% SANED + 2% occupational hazards for Saudis; 2% hazards only for expats) is shown for information and never reduces your net.

Worked example

Saudi employee on the new system (Jul 2026 – Jun 2027), basic SAR 10,000 + housing SAR 2,500. Contributory wage = SAR 12,500. Deduction = 12,500 × 10.75% = SAR 1,343.75, so net pay is SAR 11,156.25 and the employer pays a further SAR 1,593.75 on top. An expat on the identical package has no deduction at all: net SAR 12,500, with the employer paying only the 2% hazards branch (SAR 250).

Frequently asked questions

What if my basic plus housing is above SAR 45,000?

The base is capped at SAR 45,000. On a combined SAR 50,000, the new-system 2026-27 deduction is 10.75% × 45,000 = SAR 4,837.50 — not 10.75% of SAR 50,000.

Which employee option should I select?

The one matching your GOSI registration. If you were first registered before July 3, 2024, pick the old-system option (9.75%) whatever the year. New-system employees pick the period that contains today's date — the default is Jul 2026 – Jun 2027 at 10.75%.

Do transport and other allowances escape GOSI?

In this calculation, yes. Only basic salary and housing allowance form the contributory wage; other allowances increase your gross and net pay but not the GOSI deduction.

How much lower was the old system for the same pay?

On a contributory wage of SAR 12,500, the old system deducts 9.75% = SAR 1,218.75, against SAR 1,343.75 on the new system in 2026-27 — SAR 125 a month more, and the gap widens as the new-system rate steps up each July.

Does my employer's GOSI contribution come out of my pay?

No. The employer's contribution is a cost on top of your gross salary. Your net pay is reduced only by your own employee deduction.
