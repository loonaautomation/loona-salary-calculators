How it is calculated

A electricians earning the national median salary of $63,190 a year in California takes home $51,110.12 a year ($4,259.18 a month) after federal and California income tax.

The salary figure is the NATIONAL median from the BLS OEWS May 2025 estimates (SOC 47-2111) — state medians are published separately by BLS and are not used here. This page shows what the national median is worth after California’s tax, so you can compare states on the same pay.

On that salary, the federal tax is $5,402.80 and the California tax is $1,843.05 a year. Social Security and Medicare take $4,834.03.

How it works — the formula

Federal tax is computed on taxable income (gross minus the 2026 standard deduction of $16,100) using the 2026 IRS brackets. California tax is computed on the state’s 2026 table (Tax Foundation, as of Jan 1, 2026). Social Security is 6.2% of wages up to the 2026 wage base of $184,500; Medicare is 1.45% of all wages, plus 0.9% on wages above $200,000. Local (city/county) taxes are not included.
