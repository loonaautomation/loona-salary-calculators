How it is calculated

  • INPS contributions (employee): 9.19% of gross for a standard private-sector employee, charged up to the 2026 contribution ceiling of €122,295, plus an extra 1% on the slice of pay above €56,224. Your employer pays roughly a further 30% on top — never deducted from you.
  • IRPEF 2026 (Budget Law 2026): 23% to €28,000; 33% from €28,001 to €50,000 (cut from 35%); 43% above €50,000. The taxable base is gross minus your INPS contributions.
  • Employment tax credit (detrazione per lavoro dipendente): €1,955 at taxable incomes up to €15,000, then tapering on a statutory formula to zero at €50,000. It is subtracted from the IRPEF, and it is why lower salaries keep more than the headline brackets suggest.
  • Not included (why this is an estimate): the regional surtax (addizionale regionale, roughly 1.2–3.3%) and municipal surtax (0–0.9%) depend on where you live, and the trattamento integrativo top-up for lower incomes is ignored. Expect a real net roughly 1–3% below this figure.

Worked example: on €40,000 gross — INPS €3,676, taxable base €36,324, IRPEF €9,186.92 minus a €1,187.33 employment credit = €7,999.59, net €28,324.41 a year before local surtaxes. The live example above uses €35,000.00: INPS €3,216.50, IRPEF €6,107.03, net €25,676.47.

Frequently asked questions

What are the IRPEF brackets in Italy for 2026?

Three brackets: 23% on taxable income up to €28,000, 33% from €28,001 to €50,000, and 43% above €50,000. The middle rate was cut from 35% to 33% by the 2026 Budget Law (Law 199/2025). Taxable income is your gross salary minus employee INPS contributions, and the employment tax credit is then subtracted from the tax.

How much are INPS employee contributions?

9.19% of gross pay for a typical private-sector employee (the IVS rate), up to the annual contribution ceiling (€122,295 in 2026), with an additional 1% on earnings above €56,224. Sector funds (for example for executives or journalists) use different rates.

Why is my real Italian net lower than this calculator shows?

Mainly the local surtaxes: your region adds roughly 1.2–3.3% and your municipality up to about 0.9% on top of IRPEF, and they are not modeled here because they depend on your exact address. Family-related credits and the trattamento integrativo can push the other way for lower incomes.

What is the detrazione per lavoro dipendente?

The employment tax credit: €1,955 if your taxable income is €15,000 or less, tapering through the €15,000–€50,000 range on a fixed formula and reaching zero at €50,000. It is applied automatically in payroll and is included in this calculator.

What is the tredicesima?

The thirteenth monthly payment: most Italian contracts pay the annual salary (RAL) in 13 installments — 12 monthly plus an extra one in December (some contracts have a 14th). The annual net is the same; divide by 13 instead of 12 for the per-payment figure on such contracts.

How it works — the formula

Net = gross − INPS − IRPEF. INPS = 9.19% of gross (capped at €122,295) plus 1% of any pay above €56,224. IRPEF is charged on (gross − INPS) at 23% to €28,000, 33% to €50,000 and 43% above, and then the employment tax credit is subtracted: €1,955 up to €15,000 of taxable income; €1,910 + €1,190 × (28,000 − income) ÷ 13,000 between €15,000 and €28,000; €1,910 × (50,000 − income) ÷ 22,000 between €28,000 and €50,000; zero above €50,000. Regional and municipal surtaxes are deliberately excluded — see the estimate note.

Worked example

Gross €35,000.00 a year. INPS = 9.19% × €35,000.00 = €3,216.50. Taxable base = €35,000.00 − €3,216.50. IRPEF on that base at 23%/33%/43%, minus the employment credit for the income level, gives €6,107.03. Net = €25,676.47 a year — €2,139.71 a month over 12 payments (divide by 13 if your contract pays a tredicesima). Effective deduction rate at this salary: €26.64% before local surtaxes.

Frequently asked questions

How much difference do the local surtaxes make?

On €40,000 in a mid-rate region and city (say about 2% combined), roughly €700–€800 a year of extra tax. High-surtax areas such as Lazio or large cities sit at the top of the range; a few regions are near the bottom.

Does the 2026 middle-bracket cut help me?

If your taxable income passes €28,000, yes: every euro between €28,000 and €50,000 is now taxed at 33% instead of 35% — worth up to €440 a year at €50,000 of taxable income versus the old rate.

Are contributions really capped at €122,295?

For employees first insured after 1995, yes — INPS contributions stop at the annual ceiling (massimale), with the extra 1% applying between €56,224 and the ceiling. Above it, only IRPEF (43%) and the surtaxes bite.

I am moving to Italy — is there a special regime?

Possibly: the impatriate regime can exempt a large share of employment income for qualifying new residents, which would beat this standard calculation by a wide margin. It has strict conditions and has been narrowed in recent years — take advice before relying on it.
