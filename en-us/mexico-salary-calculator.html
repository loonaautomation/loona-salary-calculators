How it is calculated

A MX$360,000 salary in Mexico in 2026 nets MX$305,851 a year (MX$25,488 a month) after income tax and the social contributions the calculator models. Progressive federal ISR, 11-band annual tariff (SAT Anexo 8). Thresholds raised ~13% for 2026 after years frozen — older tables showing first band MXN 8,952 are pre-2026 and WRONG for 2026.

How it works — the formula

Income tax is computed progressively: 1.92% on income from 0 to 10,135; 6.4% on income from 10,135 to 86,022; 10.88% on income from 86,022 to 151,176; 16% on income from 151,176 to 175,736; 17.92% on income from 175,736 to 210,404; 21.36% on income from 210,404 to 424,354; 23.52% on income from 424,354 to 668,840; 30% on income from 668,840 to 1,276,926; 32% on income from 1,276,926 to 1,702,568; 34% on income from 1,702,568 to 5,107,704; 35% on income from 5,107,704 and above. The first MX$407 is tax-free. No social-security deduction is modeled (see the notes for what is excluded). Net pay is gross minus income tax and the social contributions modeled. This is an estimate for a single earner with no other income and no dependents — your actual pay may differ.
