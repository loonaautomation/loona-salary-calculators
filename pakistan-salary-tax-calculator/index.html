How it is calculated

Salary tax is worked out on your annual taxable salary using the salaried-class slabs (salary is more than 75% of taxable income), then divided by 12 — that is the amount your employer withholds each month under section 149.

Tax Year 2026-27 (Finance Act 2026): 0% to Rs 600,000 · 1% to Rs 1,200,000 · Rs 6,000 + 11% to Rs 2,200,000 · Rs 116,000 + 20% to Rs 3,200,000 · Rs 316,000 + 25% to Rs 4,100,000 · Rs 541,000 + 29% to Rs 5,600,000 · Rs 976,000 + 32% to Rs 7,000,000 · Rs 1,424,000 + 35% above. The section 4AB surcharge no longer applies to salaried persons.

Tax Year 2025-26 (Finance Act 2025): same first bands, then Rs 116,000 + 23% to Rs 3,200,000 · Rs 346,000 + 30% to Rs 4,100,000 · Rs 616,000 + 35% above — plus a 9% surcharge on the tax where income exceeds Rs 10 million.

Worked example: Rs 250,000/month = Rs 3,000,000/year. TY 2026-27 tax = Rs 116,000 + 20% × Rs 800,000 = Rs 276,000 per year = Rs 23,000 per month. (On the old 2025-26 table it would be Rs 300,000 — the new middle-band rates are lower.)

Frequently asked questions

What are the TY 2026-27 salary slabs?

See the table in "How it is calculated" above — the headline changes from last year are the 20% and 25% middle bands and two new bands at 29% and 32% before the 35% top rate.

Is there still a surcharge on salary?

No — for salaried persons it was abolished from TY 2026-27. In TY 2025-26 it was 9% of the tax above Rs 10 million of income (not 10%: that was TY 2025, and remains the non-salaried rate).

Tax on Rs 100,000 per month?

Rs 1,200,000 a year → Rs 6,000 a year → about Rs 500 a month in TY 2026-27.

Which year should I pick?

2026-27 is current (in force since 1 July 2026) and is the default. Pick 2025-26 only for last year's salary.

Do these slabs cover business income?

No — these are the salaried-class slabs (salary > 75% of taxable income). Non-salaried income uses a different table.

How it works — the formula

The calculator annualises your monthly salary (× 12) and runs the full year through the slab table for the tax year you select, then divides the annual tax by 12 to get the monthly withholding under section 149. The year selector defaults to Tax Year 2026-27 (Finance Act 2026): 0% to Rs 600,000 · Rs 0 + 1% to Rs 1,200,000 · Rs 6,000 + 11% to Rs 2,200,000 · Rs 116,000 + 20% to Rs 3,200,000 · Rs 316,000 + 25% to Rs 4,100,000 · Rs 541,000 + 29% to Rs 5,600,000 · Rs 976,000 + 32% to Rs 7,000,000 · Rs 1,424,000 + 35% above that. In each band the tax is a fixed base amount plus the band rate on the slice of salary inside the band. Tax Year 2025-26 uses the older six-band table (23% and 30% middle bands, 35% above Rs 4,100,000) and adds a 9% surcharge on the tax when annual salary exceeds Rs 10 million; the 2026-27 table has no salaried surcharge at all.

Worked example

Salary Rs 300,000 a month = Rs 3,600,000 a year, Tax Year 2026-27. The salary lands in the Rs 3,200,000–4,100,000 band: tax = Rs 316,000 + 25% × Rs 400,000 = Rs 416,000 a year, so Rs 34,666.67 is withheld each month and take-home pay is Rs 265,333.33 a month — an effective rate of 11.56%. The same salary on the 2025-26 table would be taxed Rs 466,000, exactly Rs 50,000 more.

Frequently asked questions

I receive a bonus or arrears — how are they taxed?

Add them to your annual salary before calculating. The slab table applies to the year's total salary, so a large one-off payment can push part of that year's income into a higher band.

Does this include EOBI, provident fund or other payroll deductions?

No. This calculator works out income tax only, so the net figure shown is your salary minus income tax. Any EOBI or fund deductions come off separately in your actual payslip.

What does the effective rate mean?

Your total tax divided by your gross salary. It is always lower than your top band rate, because the lower slices of your salary are taxed at the lower band rates — in the example above the top band is 25% but the effective rate is 11.56%.

How big was the old surcharge at very high salaries?

At Rs 1,000,000 a month (Rs 12,000,000 a year), Tax Year 2025-26 gives Rs 3,685,290 including Rs 304,290 of surcharge, while 2026-27 gives Rs 3,174,000 with no surcharge — a difference of Rs 511,290 a year.

My salary changed part-way through the year — what do I enter?

Enter the total salary for the whole tax year (add the months at each rate together). The slabs apply to the annual total, which is why this calculator works in annual figures first.

Is the first Rs 600,000 always tax-free?

Yes, in both years on this page. The 0% band covers the first Rs 600,000 for every salaried person; the 1% rate applies only to the slice between Rs 600,000 and Rs 1,200,000.
