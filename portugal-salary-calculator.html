How it is calculated

A €30,000.00 salary in Portugal in 2026 nets €21,889.32 a year (€1,824.11 a month) after income tax and the social contributions the calculator models. Progressive IRS, 9 mainland brackets (CIRS Art. 68, State Budget Law 73-A/2025). Plus solidarity surcharge 2.5% on taxable income EUR 80,000-250,000 and 5% above. Madeira/Azores have regional adaptations.

Income tax on that salary is €4,810.68 and social contributions are €3,300.00. 11% of gross salary The table below shows the same calculation at half and double the salary, so you can see how the tax grows.

The rates used are the Art. 68 table (12.5%–48%). In October 2026 the government reportedly approved a decree-law cutting the first six rates for 2026 income, but the published decree text was not found — if that cut is confirmed, the rates on this page will be updated. The solidarity surcharge (2.5% on taxable income EUR 80,000–250,000, 5% above) is included.

How it works — the formula

Income tax is computed progressively: 12.5% on income from 0 to 8,342; 15.7% on income from 8,342 to 12,587; 21.2% on income from 12,587 to 17,838; 24.1% on income from 17,838 to 23,089; 31.1% on income from 23,089 to 29,397; 34.9% on income from 29,397 to 43,090; 43.1% on income from 43,090 to 46,566; 44.6% on income from 46,566 to 86,634; 48% on income from 86,634 and above. The first €4,587.00 is tax-free. Social contributions: 11.00% of gross pay. Net pay is gross minus income tax and the social contributions modelled. This is an estimate for a single earner with no other income and no dependents — your actual pay may differ.
