How it is calculated

At $44 an hour, a full-time year (40 hours a week × 52 weeks = 2,080 hours) pays $91,520.00 before tax. Federal income tax on that salary is $11,304.40 (single filer, 2026 standard deduction of $16,100), Social Security is $5,674.24 and Medicare is $1,327.04 — leaving $73,214.32 a year, $6,101.19 a month and $1,407.97 a week.

These are federal figures only. Your state takes its own share on top — the state pages on this site work it out for all 50 states — and a 401(k), health insurance or other pre-tax deduction lowers the taxable income and so the tax. Overtime (hours past 40 a week, paid at 1.5×) is not included here; the Hours calculators on Loona Hours work that out.

How it works — the formula

Federal tax is computed on taxable income (gross minus the standard deduction) using the 2026 IRS brackets, applied progressively — each band of income is taxed at its own rate. Social Security is 6.2% of wages up to the 2026 wage base; Medicare is 1.45% of all wages, plus 0.9% on wages above $200,000 (single) or $250,000 (married filing jointly). Net pay is gross minus federal tax, Social Security, Medicare and state tax. This is an estimate for a single earner with no other income, no dependents and no itemised deductions — your actual pay may differ.
