How it is calculated

Etsy's percentage fees are charged on the total the buyer pays for the item plus the shipping you charge — not just the item price. Transaction fee = total × 6.5%. Payment processing (Etsy Payments, US) = total × 3% + $0.25. Regulatory operating fee (US) = total × 0.59%. On top of that comes the flat $0.20 listing fee, and — only when a sale actually came from an Offsite Ad — the 12% or 15% Offsite Ads fee on the same total.

Frequently asked questions

What fees does Etsy charge in 2026?

For a US shop the standard stack is: a 6.5% transaction fee on the item price plus shipping charged, a $0.20 listing fee per item, Etsy Payments processing of 3% + $0.25, and a 0.59% regulatory operating fee. If the sale came from Etsy's Offsite Ads, add 12% or 15% on top. On a typical $55 order that is about $6.00 in fees before any Offsite Ads fee.

Is the Etsy transaction fee charged on shipping too?

Yes. The 6.5% transaction fee — and the payment processing and regulatory fees — are calculated on the total amount of the sale, which is the item price plus the shipping you charge the buyer. That is why offering “free shipping” by folding postage into the item price does not reduce Etsy's percentage fees.

When is the Offsite Ads fee 12% and when is it 15%?

Shops that made $10,000 or more in the previous 365 days pay 12% and must take part. Smaller shops pay 15% per attributed sale but can opt out of Offsite Ads entirely. The fee is only charged when a buyer clicked an Offsite Ad for your listing within the attribution window before buying — never on ordinary organic sales.

Why is my Etsy profit so much lower than my price suggests?

Because the fees stack and almost all of them are charged on shipping as well as the item. On the worked example on this page — a $50 item with $5 shipping — fees alone take about $6.00, roughly 11% of what the buyer paid, before your product and postage costs. Raising the price slightly or charging realistic shipping usually matters more than it first appears.

Are Etsy fees the same outside the United States?

No. The 6.5% transaction fee and $0.20 listing fee are global, but payment processing rates and the regulatory operating fee differ by country — the regulatory fee also applies in countries such as the UK, France, Germany, Italy, Spain, Canada, Australia and Türkiye at different percentages. That is why every rate in this calculator is an editable field: set them to your own country's rates and the maths stays correct.

How it works — the formula

The calculator starts from the total amount of the sale: total = item price + shipping charged to the buyer. Transaction fee = total × transaction %, regulatory fee = total × regulatory %, payment processing = total × processing % + the fixed processing amount, and the Offsite Ads fee = total × 12% or 15% when it applies. Add the flat listing fee to get total fees. Profit = total − total fees − your product cost − your shipping cost. Results are rounded to two decimals for display.

Frequently asked questions

How long does the $0.20 listing fee last?

Each listing lasts four months or until the item sells. For multi-quantity listings the $0.20 fee is charged again each time an additional unit sells. This calculator includes one listing fee per order, which matches a single-item sale.

Does Etsy charge its fees on sales tax?

No — the percentage fees are calculated on the item price plus the shipping you charge, not on sales tax that Etsy collects and remits on your behalf in states and countries where it is required to do so.
