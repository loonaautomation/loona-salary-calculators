Why these calculators are different

Most gratuity and salary calculators on the web still use outdated rules — some still apply the UAE's pre-2022 resignation reductions that were abolished in February 2022, and many Pakistan calculators still show last year's slabs. Every Loona calculator shows the formula it uses, the law it comes from, and the date it was last verified, so you can check the maths yourself.
