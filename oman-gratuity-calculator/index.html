Oman Gratuity Calculator

End-of-service gratuity under Article 61 of the Omani Labour Law (Royal Decree 53/2023). Because the law changed on 1 August 2023, this calculator splits your service at that date and applies each law to its own period.

In short: service from 1 August 2023 earns one month of your last basic wage per year. Earlier service earns 15 days per year for the first three years and one month per year after, under the old law. There is no minimum-service threshold and no reduction for resigning.

How it is calculated

  • New law (service from 1 Aug 2023), Article 61 of RD 53/2023: at least 1 month's basic wage per year, pro-rated, from the very first year — no minimum-service threshold and no resignation reduction.
  • Old law (service before 1 Aug 2023), RD 35/2003: 15 days' basic wage per year for the first 3 years, then 1 month per year. Both parts are calculated on your last basic wage; allowances never count.
  • Formula: Gratuity = Basic × (0.5 × min(pre-2023 years, 3) + max(pre-2023 years − 3, 0)) + Basic × post-2023 years.
  • Reading note: the 15-days-for-the-first-3-years treatment of pre-2023 service is the reading supported by most sources; one summary of the Ministry's worked example describes pre-law service at a flat half-month per year. For long pre-2023 service, confirm the transitional treatment with the Ministry of Labour.
  • Coming change: the Social Protection Law (RD 52/2023) will replace expatriate gratuity with an employer-funded savings scheme (9% of basic wage monthly). The start date must be announced by 19 July 2027; until then, Article 61 gratuity keeps accruing.
  • This gratuity applies to workers outside the Social Protection Law — in practice expatriates. Omani nationals are covered by the Social Protection Fund.

Worked example: basic salary OMR 1,000, service 1 Jan 2020 – 1 Jan 2026. Pre-Aug-2023 service ≈ 3.58 years → 0.5 × 3 + 0.58 = 2.08 months = OMR 2,081; post-Aug-2023 service ≈ 2.42 years → OMR 2,420; total ≈ OMR 4,501 (exact figure in the calculator above, using your precise dates).

Frequently asked questions

How is gratuity calculated in Oman?

From 1 Aug 2023: one month of last basic wage per year. Before that date: 15 days per year for the first three years, one month per year after — both parts on the last basic wage.

Basic salary or full salary?

Basic wage only, for both parts. Allowances are excluded.

Does resigning reduce it?

No. The 2023 law has no resignation reduction and no minimum-service threshold.

Who does it apply to?

Workers not covered by the Social Protection Law — in practice, expatriates. Omani nationals are in the Social Protection Fund system.

Is gratuity being replaced?

Yes — an employer-funded savings scheme (9% of basic monthly) under the Social Protection Law, starting on a date to be announced by 19 July 2027. Until it starts, Article 61 gratuity continues.

How it works — the formula

Article 61 of the Labour Law (RD 53/2023) pays one month of your last basic salary per year of service — but service before 1 August 2023 is still valued under the old law (RD 35/2003): half a month per year for the first three pre-2023 years, and one month per year after that. The calculator splits your dates at 1 August 2023, converts each part to years (days ÷ 365.25), applies the two formulas to your last basic wage, and adds the parts together.

Formula: Gratuity = Basic × (0.5 × min(pre-Aug-2023 years, 3) + max(pre-Aug-2023 years − 3, 0)) + Basic × post-Aug-2023 years

Worked example

Last basic salary OMR 850, service 1 June 2021 to 31 May 2026 — 5.00 years in total. Pre-August-2023 service is 2.17 years, all inside the first-three-years band, so it pays half a month per year: OMR 920.397. Post-August-2023 service is 2.83 years at a full month per year: OMR 2,406.297. Total gratuity: OMR 3,326.694.

Frequently asked questions

I joined after 1 August 2023 — which formula applies to me?

Only the new one: one month of basic wage per year, pro-rated, from your very first year — the pre-2023 part of the calculation stays at zero. There is no minimum-service threshold, and resigning does not reduce the amount.

Why is my service split in August 2023 when I never changed jobs?

Because the law changed mid-service. RD 53/2023 took effect on 1 August 2023 and raised the accrual to a full month per year; service before that date keeps the old RD 35/2003 valuation, and service after it gets the new rate. Both parts are calculated on your last basic wage.

What if most of my service was before August 2023?

Then most of your gratuity is valued at the old rates — half a month per year for the first three of those years, and a full month only for pre-2023 service beyond three years. In the worked example above, the 2.17 pre-2023 years earn noticeably less per year than the 2.83 newer years for exactly this reason.

Are part-years and part-months paid?

Yes. Both service periods are converted from your exact dates (days ÷ 365.25), so a leftover seven months counts as about 0.58 of a year in whichever period it falls.

My basic salary rose over the years — is each period valued at the salary of its time?

No. Both the old-law and the new-law parts are calculated on your last basic salary, as Article 61 prescribes — so a late raise increases the value of your whole service, including the pre-2023 years.
