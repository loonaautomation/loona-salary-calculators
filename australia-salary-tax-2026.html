How it works — the formula

Income tax is computed progressively: 0% on income from 0 to 18,200; 15% on income from 18,200 to 45,000; 30% on income from 45,000 to 135,000; 37% on income from 135,000 to 190,000; 45% on income from 190,000 and above. No social-security deduction is modelled (see the notes for what is excluded). Net pay is gross minus income tax and the social contributions modelled. This is an estimate for a single earner with no other income and no dependents — your actual pay may differ.
