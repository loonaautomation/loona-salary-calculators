How We Work

How these calculators are built, sourced and kept current.

Calculations run in your browser

Every calculator on this site does its maths in your own web browser. The salary figures and dates you enter never leave your device — they are not uploaded, stored or shared, and there is no account to create. The site is free and supported by advertising.

Figures from published official rates

Each calculator is built from the published law, rate table or official guidance for its country, and the page states what it uses — for example a labour law article, a tax slab table or a statutory minimum wage. Where an official calculation is more detailed than a published table allows, the page labels its result an estimate instead of pretending to be a payslip.

Updated periodically

Tax and wage figures change on government calendars — typically each January in Europe and at budget time elsewhere. We re-check calculators against newly published official rates and update pages when those rates change. If you spot a figure that no longer matches an official source, the Feedback page is the fastest way to get it fixed.
