Zakat Calculator

Work out your Zakat the careful way: gold and silver valued at today's rates (with the karat adjustment for jewellery), plus cash, stock and receivables, minus debts — checked against the nisab before the 2.5% is applied.

In short: Zakat is 2.5% of your net zakatable wealth, due when that wealth has stayed at or above the nisab — 52.5 tola of silver (the usual benchmark for mixed wealth) or 7.5 tola of gold — for one lunar year. How it is calculated

  • Formula: Zakatable = gold value + silver value + cash & bank + business stock + receivables − debts due now. If zakatable ≥ nisab, Zakat = 2.5% × zakatable; otherwise no Zakat is due.
  • Nisab: 87.48 g of gold (7.5 tola) or 612.36 g of silver (52.5 tola). For mixed wealth, the silver nisab is the benchmark most commonly used in Pakistan (Hanafi practice); the gold nisab is available above for gold-only wealth. 1 tola = 11.664 g.
  • Gold valuation: weight in tola × today's 24K rate × (karat ÷ 24) — so 22-karat jewellery is valued on its pure-gold content. Silver is valued at the pure-silver rate.
  • Hawl: Zakat is due when the wealth has remained at or above the nisab for one lunar year — the calculator checks the amount; the year is yours to confirm.
  • Not counted: your home, car, furniture and personal-use items are not zakatable. Business stock is valued at its sale value.
  • Context: for Zakat year 1446-47 AH the officially notified bank-deduction nisab in Pakistan was Rs 503,529 (16 February 2026) — that figure governs only the automatic bank deduction on 1 Ramadan, not your personal obligation, which this calculator works out from your actual wealth.

Worked example: 10 tola of 24K gold at Rs 200,000 per tola = Rs 2,000,000; cash Rs 500,000; debts Rs 100,000 → net zakatable Rs 2,400,000, well above the silver nisab of Rs 131,250 (52.5 × Rs 2,500) → Zakat = Rs 60,000.

Frequently asked questions

How is Zakat calculated?

2.5% of net zakatable wealth (gold + silver + cash + stock + receivables − debts due), once it reaches the nisab and a lunar year has passed.

What is the nisab?

87.48 g gold (7.5 tola) or 612.36 g silver (52.5 tola). Mixed wealth usually uses the silver nisab in Pakistan; gold-only wealth uses the gold nisab.

Is Zakat due on jewellery?

Schools differ — Hanafi practice generally counts gold and silver jewellery; other schools exempt jewellery in regular use. Enter what your school counts and follow your scholar's guidance.

How is non-24K gold valued?

On pure-gold content: weight × (karat ÷ 24) × the 24K rate. The calculator adjusts automatically.

What about the bank deduction nisab?

Banks deduct on 1 Ramadan from accounts above the annually notified figure (Rs 503,529 for 1446-47 AH). That is separate from your personal Zakat on total wealth.

How it works — the formula

Everything is first valued in cash. Gold is valued on its pure content: (grams ÷ 11.664) × today's 24K rate per tola × (karat ÷ 24), and silver at (grams ÷ 11.664) × the silver rate per tola. Zakatable wealth = gold value + silver value + cash and bank balances + business stock + receivables − debts due now. The nisab check uses the benchmark you select: the silver nisab, 52.5 tola × the silver rate, by default for mixed wealth, or the gold nisab, 7.5 tola × the gold rate. If zakatable wealth reaches the nisab, Zakat = 2.5% of it; below the nisab, nothing is due.

Worked example

Gold 58.32 g (5 tola) of 24K at Rs 200,000 per tola = Rs 1,000,000. Silver 116.64 g (10 tola) at Rs 2,500 per tola = Rs 25,000. Cash Rs 400,000, business stock Rs 200,000 and receivables Rs 50,000 bring total assets to Rs 1,675,000; debts due of Rs 75,000 leave zakatable wealth of Rs 1,600,000. The silver nisab at these rates is Rs 131,250, so the nisab is met and Zakat = 2.5% × 1,600,000 = Rs 40,000. (On the gold nisab — Rs 1,500,000 at these rates — it would also be due, and the amount would be the same.)

Frequently asked questions

Why does the nisab benchmark change whether Zakat is due?

Because the two nisabs are worth very different amounts. At the rates in the example, the silver nisab is Rs 131,250 while the gold nisab is Rs 1,500,000 — wealth between those two figures owes Zakat on the silver benchmark but not on the gold one. The rate itself is 2.5% either way.

How many grams are in one tola?

11.664 grams. So the gold nisab of 7.5 tola is 87.48 g, the silver nisab of 52.5 tola is 612.36 g, and the 58.32 g in the example is exactly 5 tola.

Which debts can I deduct?

The field in this calculator is debts due now — amounts you are currently obliged to pay. How to treat future instalments of a longer loan is a point scholars differ on, so enter what is due now and follow your scholar's guidance for the rest.

Should I include money people owe me?

The receivables field is for money owed to you that you expect back, and the calculator counts it as zakatable. If a debt owed to you is doubtful, that is another point schools treat differently — follow your scholar's guidance on whether to enter it.

What if my wealth fell below the nisab during the year?

Zakat needs the wealth to stay at or above the nisab for a lunar year (hawl). This calculator checks the amount you enter today against the nisab; whether the full year condition is met is yours to confirm.
