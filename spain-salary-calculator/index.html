How it is calculated

  • Social security (employee): 6.50% of the contribution base for permanent contracts — 4.70% common contingencies + 1.55% unemployment + 0.10% vocational training + 0.15% MEI (2026 rates, Orden PJC/297/2026). Temporary contracts pay 1.60% unemployment, i.e. 6.55% total. The base is your gross salary, floored at about €17,094 a year and capped at €61,214.40.
  • IRPF taxable base = gross − social security − €2,000 (standard employment-expenses deduction).
  • Standard IRPF scale (state + default regional): 19% to €12,450; 24% to €20,200; 30% to €35,200; 37% to €60,000; 45% to €300,000; 47% above.
  • Personal minimum: the tax on €5,550 at the scale (€1,054.50) is subtracted — the standard calculator treatment of the mínimo personal.

Regional warning: each autonomous community sets its own regional half of the scale. Combined top rates run from ~45% (Madrid) to ~54% (Valencian Community), with Catalonia around 50%. This calculator uses the standard scale throughout; if you live in a high-tax community your net will be lower. The Basque Country and Navarra run separate foral systems and are out of scope.

Worked example: gross €30,000, permanent contract → social security €1,950; taxable base €26,050; scale tax €5,980.50 minus €1,054.50 personal minimum = IRPF €4,926; net €23,124 a year.

Frequently asked questions

How is net salary calculated in Spain?

Gross minus 6.50% employee social security (on the capped contribution base), then IRPF on the standard scale over (gross − social security − €2,000), less the tax on the €5,550 personal minimum.

Why 14 payments?

Many contracts split the same annual pay into 12 monthly payments plus two extra (June and December). Annual net is identical; the per-payment amount changes.

Is IRPF the same across Spain?

No — each autonomous community sets its regional half. This calculator uses the standard scale; Madrid is lower, Valencia and Catalonia higher. Basque Country and Navarra are separate systems.

What is the social security ceiling?

€5,101.20 per month (€61,214.40 per year) in 2026 — earnings above it pay no further employee contributions.
