Bahrain Indemnity Calculator

Leaving indemnity under Article 116 of the Bahraini Labour Law — the end-of-service payment for workers who are not covered by the Social Insurance Law, which in practice means most expatriate employees.

In short: Bahrain leaving indemnity is half a month's wage for each of the first three years of service and one month's wage for every year after that, on your most recent wage (basic plus social allowance). There is no cap and no reduction for resigning.

How it is calculated

  • Who it covers: workers not subject to the Social Insurance Law (Article 116, Legislative Decree 36 of 2012) — in practice expatriates, and Bahrainis paid above the social-insurance wage ceiling. If you are inside the SIO pension system, this indemnity does not apply.
  • Rate: half a month's wage (15 days) per year for the first 3 years, then one month's wage per year. Part-years are pro-rated.
  • Wage base: your most recent wage under Article 47 — basic wage plus social allowance. Housing, transport and other allowances are excluded.
  • No cap and no resignation reduction.
  • Formula: Indemnity = Wage × (0.5 × min(years, 3) + max(years − 3, 0)).

Worked example: wage BHD 800, 5 years of service → 800 × (0.5 × 3 + 2) = 800 × 3.5 = BHD 2,800.

How it works — the formula

Article 116 of the Labour Law (Legislative Decree 36 of 2012) pays a leaving indemnity to employees who are outside the Social Insurance Law. The amount depends on only two things: your most recent monthly wage — basic wage plus social allowance, as Article 47 defines it — and your length of service. The first three years earn half a month's wage each; every year after that earns a full month's wage. Service is counted in days and converted to years (days ÷ 365.25), so part-years are paid pro-rata, and there is no ceiling on the total.

Formula: Indemnity = Wage × (0.5 × min(years, 3) + 1 × max(years − 3, 0))

Worked example

Most recent wage BHD 650 (basic plus social allowance), joined 15 June 2019, last working day 14 June 2026. That is 2,556 days of service, which the calculator converts to 7.00 years. First 3 years: 650 × 0.5 × 3 = BHD 975.000. The remaining ≈4.00 years at a full month per year add BHD 2,598.665. Total leaving indemnity: BHD 3,573.665.

Frequently asked questions

How is a part-year of service counted?

Pro-rata, by days. Your joining and leaving dates are converted to years at 365.25 days per year and the formula is applied to that fraction — so two and a half years of service earn 1.25 months of wage (0.5 × 2.5), not a rounded-down two years.

My salary went up last year — which wage is used for my earlier years?

Your most recent wage is used for the whole period. The indemnity is built on the wage as defined in Article 47 at the end of service, so earlier years are not recalculated at the lower wages you earned then — a late-career raise lifts the entire indemnity.

Is there a minimum length of service before any indemnity is due?

Article 116 sets no minimum-service threshold, and this calculator applies the formula from your first day, pro-rated by days. That differs from the UAE and Qatar formulas used on this site, which pay nothing for service under one year.

I worked exactly 3 years — how much do I get?

Exactly 1.5 months of wage: all of the first three years earn the half-month rate, and only service beyond the third anniversary earns the full month. At a wage of BHD 650 that is BHD 975.000; the same wage over 10 years builds to 8.5 months of wage — BHD 5,525.

Does this calculator include pay for unused annual leave?

No. It calculates only the Article 116 leaving indemnity. Pay for unused leave, notice-period amounts and any other final-settlement items are separate entitlements and are not included in the figure shown.
