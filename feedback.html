Feedback & Bug Reports

Tell us about a wrong figure, a broken calculator or a country you want covered.

Send feedback by email

Feedback and bug reports go by email — there is no form on this page, and no form that submits nowhere. Write to loonaautomation@gmail.com; we read every message.

What to include

  • The calculator's name or page address — for example, the UAE Gratuity Calculator.
  • What happened — the figures you entered, the result you got and the result you expected.
  • The official source you checked against, if the issue is a rate or a rule.
  • Your device and browser — for example, "Android phone, Chrome".

Please do not send payslips or personal documents — a description of the figures is enough to check a calculation.

What happens next

Reports are checked against the published official rates for that country. Confirmed errors are corrected on the live page at the same address. We may not be able to reply to every message individually, but every report is checked. For other questions, see the Contact page and our FAQ.
