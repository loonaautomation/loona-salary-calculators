How it works — the formula

Income tax is computed progressively: 0% on income from 0 to 12,570; 20% on income from 12,570 to 50,270; 40% on income from 50,270 to 125,140; 45% on income from 125,140 and above. Social contributions: 8.00% on pay from 12,570 to 50,270; 2.00% on pay from 50,270 and above. Net pay is gross minus income tax and the social contributions modelled. This is an estimate for a single earner with no other income and no dependents — your actual pay may differ.
