How it works — the formula

Income tax is computed progressively: 0% on income from 0 to 375,000; 10% on income from 375,000 to 675,000; 15% on income from 675,000 to 1,075,000; 20% on income from 1,075,000 to 1,575,000; 25% on income from 1,575,000 to 3,575,000; 30% on income from 3,575,000 and above. No social-security deduction is modelled (see the notes for what is excluded). Net pay is gross minus income tax and the social contributions modelled. This is an estimate for a single earner with no other income and no dependents — your actual pay may differ.
